Observation vs. Interpretation vs. Inference

Mandatory forensic distinction enforced throughout the investigation:

Level Scientific Definition Case Application
Observation Direct byte facts from disk. Row in ledger has TXN_20240522_001, 75000.0, timestamp 1716381600.
Interpretation Technical software mechanism. QuickPay debited ₹75,000 at 2024-05-22 12:40:00 UTC to counterparty.
Inference Human investigative deduction. User transferred funds to Vikram Malhotra as part of a transaction.
Forensic Limitations & Alternative Explanations:
